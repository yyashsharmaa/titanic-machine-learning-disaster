What Sorts of People Were More Likely to Survive?

Historical & Empirical Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis answers Kaggle's core inquiry: the demographic, socio-economic, and logistical determinants of Titanic survival.

👩

Gender Priority ("Women First")

74.2% of women survived vs 18.9% of men.

The maritime evacuation code strictly enforced loading females onto lifeboats first, creating the single strongest predictive feature.

🎟️

Socio-Economic Class

63.0% in 1st Class vs 24.2% in 3rd Class.

1st class staterooms were positioned directly below the boat decks, while 3rd class passengers faced maze-like corridors and locked steerage gates.

👶

Children & "Master" Title

58.0% of children & boys survived.

Young boys carrying the title "Master" had an ~57% survival rate, in stark contrast to adult "Mr" passengers who faced an ~84% mortality rate.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Size Sweet Spot

57.8% for families of 2 to 4.

Small families aided each other during the evacuation. Solo travelers (30.4%) lacked alerts, while very large families (>4, ~15%) struggled to assemble all members.

⚓

Port of Embarkation

55.4% for Cherbourg passengers.

Cherbourg passengers had the highest survival proportion, primarily because over 50% were affluent 1st Class ticket holders.

🚪

Cabin Deck Proximity

66.7% for recorded cabin holders.

Decks B, C, D, and E held the highest survival rates. Passengers without a recorded cabin (missing indicator) had only a 30.0% survival rate.
